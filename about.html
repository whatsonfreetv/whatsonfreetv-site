What is WhatsOnFreeTV?

WhatsOnFreeTV is a free streaming TV directory built for cord-cutters. We bring together thousands of free channels from all the major FAST platforms into one easy-to-browse guide — so you can spend less time searching and more time watching. Whether you're looking for live news, classic movies, sports, or something niche, we help you find it without a cable bill or a streaming subscription.

What are FAST Channels?

FAST stands for Free Ad-Supported Streaming TV. These are live, linear channels you can watch for free on platforms like Pluto TV, Roku, Samsung TV Plus, and more — just like traditional cable TV but completely free. You get a real channel lineup with scheduled programming, supported by short ad breaks instead of a subscription fee.
